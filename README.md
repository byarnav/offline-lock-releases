# Vow for Mac: downloads

**Vow** turns off every network connection on your Mac (Wi-Fi, Ethernet, VPN connections, USB adapters and iPhone hotspots) and keeps it off until your timer ends, from 5 minutes to 24 hours.

**Website:** https://vow.byarnav.com

This repository hosts Vow's installer and the update feed the app checks for new versions. The app's source code is not here.

## Download

**[Download Vow.dmg](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/byarnav/offline-lock-releases/main/Vow.dmg)** (latest version, 1.0.26)

3 full sessions free, then a one-time purchase at [vow.byarnav.com](https://vow.byarnav.com/#pricing).

Requires **macOS 26 or later** on **Apple Silicon** (M1 or newer).

## Install

1. Open `Vow.dmg`.
2. Drag **Vow** into **Applications**.
3. Open Vow from Applications. It's signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Vow updates itself. You don't need to download new versions from here.

## What Vow does

- Turns off every connection for every app and browser, not just a list of websites
- VPN-proof: covers all network interfaces including VPN tunnels; cannot be bypassed by connecting to a VPN
- Sessions from 5 minutes to 24 hours, plus recurring schedules
- Keeps the session running when you quit the app or restart the Mac
- No instant cancel: an early unlock takes 15 more minutes
- Files, apps, localhost, local AI models and Bluetooth keep working
- No account, no analytics, no tracking
